    Frequently asked

    How does ServiceNow call bridging work?

    Your browser acts as the A-leg WebRTC client. When dialing out, your browser connects via secure SIP WebRTC directly, and Vobiz bridges the call to the destination number.

    Why is the Call button disabled?

    Calling requires both a signed-in account and an active SIP registration. Check the status badge at the top — it will turn green (READY) when ready to place calls.

    Where are call records and recordings saved?

    Completed calls are automatically synced to the ServiceNow interaction table with duration, caller ID, and a secure recording playback link.
